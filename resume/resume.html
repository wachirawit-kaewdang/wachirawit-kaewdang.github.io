Stock Price Direction Prediction – LSTM + Self-Attention with Explainable AISenior project (CS403)

github.com/wachirawit-kaewdang/Project-CS403

  • Built a deep learning pipeline predicting 3-day price direction for 10 US stocks across 5 sectors (2020–2025), from 27 engineered features: momentum and volatility, moving averages, RSI / MACD / Stochastic, volume and OBV, S&P 500 market context and bond yield.
  • Architecture: LSTM with self-attention (3 layers, hidden size 128) over a 60-day window, trained with Focal Loss and class weights, and compared with Random Forest, Logistic Regression, a soft-voting ensemble and a VIX feature set.
  • Compared SHAP (KernelExplainer) with Pearson correlation: SHAP ranked bond yield top-5 for 6 of 10 stocks (#1 for JPM and AMZN); correlation agreed for JPM but split for others (TSLA: 2nd by SHAP, 15th by correlation). Overall rank gaps of 5–12 positions were close to the 9 expected by chance, which we documented.
  • Evaluated results against a naive baseline: on 7 of 10 stocks the tuned model collapsed to always predicting “up”, so its F1 matched an always-up model; the ensemble’s threshold-free ROC-AUC averaged 0.568, showing short-term direction is close to random. Identified threshold and early-stopping leakage and documented fixes.
  • My contribution: data collection and preparation, training approach and model architecture, joint SHAP analysis, and the baseline check. Two-person project.
